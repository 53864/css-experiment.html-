Padding is when you add extra space on the inside of the border. The margin adds space outside of the section so that there's space in between different parts of the website. The border is what wraps around the padding and content of a section.
Width is a percentage of how wide something can be. Max-width sets a cap for how wide something can be. Min-width sets a cap for how small in width a section can be. 
Using RGBA instead of opacity makes a way for you to be able to make your section transparent without making the text or other elements inside the section transparent as well. 
Cover makes it so that you fill entire inside of the section. Contain makes it so that your whole image will be inside the section.
